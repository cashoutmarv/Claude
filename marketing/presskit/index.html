DUNGEON DRIFT

A one-finger roguelike survivor for iOS & Android.

Roguelike Survivor / Bullet Heaven Mobile-first F2P · No forced ads

Description

Dungeon Drift is a top-down auto-shooter roguelike built for one-thumb play. Move with a virtual joystick; weapons fire themselves at the nearest threat. Survive escalating waves, choose from random level-up upgrades, fight bosses every two minutes, and roll meta progression between runs — new characters, weapons, and relics earned from gold or fair-rate gacha.

“A 5–15 minute coffee-break roguelike. Die fast, learn faster, run again.”

Features

  • One-finger controls — auto-fire weapons, virtual joystick movement.
  • Short, replayable runs (5–15 minutes) with permadeath and procedural waves.
  • Random level-up upgrades each run from a deep pool of weapons and passives.
  • Meta progression: characters, weapons, and relics with 5 rarity tiers.
  • Fair gacha with hard pity at 70 pulls and selector tokens that never expire.
  • Player-respecting monetization: no banners, no interstitials, no forced video.
  • Any IAP grants permanent ad-free as a thank-you.
